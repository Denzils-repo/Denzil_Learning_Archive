One Punch Man Season 1 Episode 1

In the first episode of One-Punch Man, a monster named Vaccine Man attacks the city, but is effortlessly obliterated by an unassuming, bald hero named Saitama with a single punch. Saitama then reflects on his past, revealing that his overwhelming power cost him his hair and left him chronically bored

Attack On Titan Season 1 Episode 1

In the first episode of Attack on Titan, humanity's 100 years of peace are shattered when a massive Colossal Titan breaches the wall of Shiganshina district. Young Eren Jaeger watches in horror as his mother is devoured by a Titan, sparking his lifelong vow to eradicate them.

That Time I Got Re-Incarnated As A Slime Season 1 Episode 1

Season 1 Episode 1 ("The Storm Dragon, Veldora") introduces 37-year-old Japanese corporate worker Satoru Mikami, who is stabbed and killed by a random attacker. Reborn in a fantasy world as a blind, blue slime, he gains two unique abilities: Great Sage (an analytical internal voice) and Predator (the ability to consume objects and absorb their skills).
