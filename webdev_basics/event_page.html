2025 Anime Meet up

The 2025 Neon Horizon Anime Summit has already concluded, leaving fans with memories of a spectacular summer celebration that bridged the gap between nostalgia and innovation. Held earlier this year, the event successfully hosted live orchestral concerts featuring iconic scores from 2000s series and saw enthusiastic participation in the "Mecha Building" workshop, where remote-controlled models clashed in a fierce arena battle. The "Global Indie Stage" showcased a wave of fresh talent from international creators, while the vendor hall cleared out rare figure collections and limited-edition apparel in record time. Though the panels on animation evolution and the late-night J-Pop dance parties have ended, the community buzz from the event continues to fuel excitement for future gatherings, proving that the spirit of the summit lives on long after the final curtain fell.

2025 Retro Rewind

the 2025 Retro Rewind Anime Expo has already wrapped up, marking a nostalgic trip back to the golden eras of 80s and 90s animation. Held last autumn, this event was a throwback celebration that focused on physical media, featuring a massive "VHS & DVD Swap Meet" and a "Cel Art Gallery" displaying original hand-painted frames from classic series. The highlight was the "Retro Gaming Arcade," where fans could compete in tournaments on original arcade cabinets from titles like Street Fighter II and Dragon Ball Z: The Legend. While the event has closed its doors, the photos of the intricate cosplay and the recorded panel discussions on the history of anime dubbing remain a cherished part of the community's archive, celebrating the foundations upon which today's anime culture stands.
