Choco-Chip Cookies

Easy Recipe to make chocolate chip cookies

Ingredients

  • Cookie Dough
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the cookie dough with a splash of milk
  2. Shape the dough into small balls and place them on a baking tray.
  3. Sprinkle a little sugar and a tiny pinch of salt on top
  4. Bake at 175–180°C (350–355°F) for 10–12 minutes, or until the edges are golden.
  5. Let the cookies cool for 5–10 minutes, then serve.
